Section 01

Funding & Payment

Ghetto Movement delivers clinical EP across several funded streams plus private self-pay. Each stream sets its own appointment durations. The number that matters to you at the session is the Collect from client column — how much payment to take before they leave.

The one thing to check Before a client leaves, find their stream below and take the Collect from client amount. If it reads $0.00, there's nothing to take — it's billed for them. Rebates, claiming and plan billing are all handled in the back office, not by you at the desk.

Rule of thumb Default to the short (30 min) follow-up for routine progression. Book the long (45 min) whenever the session involves reassessment, reprogramming, or added complexity — anything more than delivering and progressing an existing program.

Short follow-up

30 min · $100 · the default

  • Established client on a stable program — progress load, tidy technique, keep momentum.
  • Single area or single goal; a straightforward run-through.
  • Client is confident and independent with their exercises.
  • Routine check-in between the bigger review points.
  • Maintenance-phase client who just needs accountability and small tweaks.

Long follow-up

45 min · $120 · when it's more than delivery

  • Re-testing / objective reassessment (strength, ROM, movement screen).
  • Writing a new program block or shifting training phase.
  • New or complex presentation — multiple areas, comorbidities, or a recent flare / setback.
  • Teaching several new movements or a significant technique rebuild.
  • First session back after a break or a run of missed sessions.
  • Need review time to prep a progress update, report, or GP letter.
Notes
  • Full fee is paid at time of service — no referral or funding required.
  • If the client has private health extras, run their card through HICAPS at reception — the rebate is applied on the spot, so they pay only their gap. No extras (or no eligible cover): collect the full fee shown above.

Section 02

Taking Payment — POS

Once you know the amount to collect, this is how to run it on the reception POS machine.

Private health rebate

HICAPS · client has extras

  1. Scan / tap the client's private health care card first.
  2. Select the appointment type and check the fee.
  3. Follow the prompts on the machine.
  4. The client pays the gap fee last.

No private health rebate

Self-pay full fee · Medicare gap

  1. Select 'Sale' and enter the amount to collect.
  2. The client pays it in full.
Which amount
  • The amount to enter is the Collect from client figure on the stream card above — the full fee for self-pay, or the gap for Medicare.
  • Streams showing $0.00 are billed for the client — nothing to run on the machine.
