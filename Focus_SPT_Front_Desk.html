Section 02

SPT Sessions vs Private Reviews

Focus SPT Sessions

What happens every session

  • Monitoring — tracking how the client moves and responds to load.
  • Feedback — real-time technique cues and coaching.
  • Progression — individual movements progressed within the session.
  • Accountability — keeping clients training consistently.

Private Reviews

Reserved for

  • New program design and full builds.
  • Major program modifications.
  • Formal testing and re-testing.
  • Goal check-ins and milestone reviews.
  • Rehab clearance to exit the EP stream.

SPT sessions keep clients moving forward day-to-day. Private reviews reset and rebuild the bigger picture.

Section 04

Front Desk Quick Script

  1. Ask: "Are you coming in with an injury, pain, or a rehab goal?" → EP stream ($159 assessment).
  2. If general training or performance goals → Coach stream ($120 assessment).
  3. Explain: their program is built in that first session — they leave with everything they need.
  4. Mention: 1 SPT session per week + homework exercises included. Optional multi-day splits available.
  5. Reviews are required to keep the program current — built into their journey from the start.
