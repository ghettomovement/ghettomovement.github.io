Stage 01

Initial Consult

The first appointment — assessment, goal-setting, and getting the client moving before they leave.

Initial duration — 45 min for Medicare, DVA, RSL & Silverchain; 60 min for private-pay & NDIS. The extra 15 minutes on a 60-min initial goes to deeper work — more in-depth VALD testing, optimising load intensities, or further assessment — which is why those two run longer than the rest.

In the session
1 · Set up the account
  • Capture the general and rebate/billing details needed to open the client's account and bill correctly — e.g. RSL sub-branch card & location, DVA veteran status, Medicare / referral details.
2 · History
  • Injury / condition history.
  • Red-flag screening.
  • Training history.
3 · Objective testing battery — in this order
  1. Active & passive ROM.
  2. Manual muscle testing.
  3. Movement screening.
  4. VALD testing (ForceDecks / DynaMo).
4 · Feedback & plan
  1. Relay the findings — results, norms, what the testing showed.
  2. Explain your view — your clinical read of what's going on.
  3. Set goals together.
  4. Agree next steps — what next week's session involves, and what they need to do before then.
After the session
  1. Book the follow-up for the following week — that session is where you cover their exercise prescription in detail.
  2. Send the VALD program within 24 hours. Tell the client to get started straight away, and that you'll walk through it together in more detail at the next session.
  3. Send the GP letter to the referring GP. DVA · Medicare · Private (if GP referred) · Silverchain

NDIS — no GP letter; communication is done through progress reports, completed when prompted. Applies at every stage.

Stage 02

Follow-up Consult

The first follow-up in detail, plus how to set session length and cadence for those that follow — routine sessions then run on your clinical judgment.

First follow-up
  1. Walk through the VALD program together.
  2. Confirm technique.
  3. Set expectations & correct intensities.
  4. Reconfirm the why through education.
  5. Relay information from their assessment where needed.
  6. Book them in for their next follow-up. Session length can vary on your clinical call, see below — adjust the fee to match (see the Fees sheet).
Choosing session length
SessionBook when
Short · 30 min Routine progression — stable program, one area, a confident and independent client, a check-in between the bigger review points.
Long · 45 min A heavier-than-routine follow-up — a flare or setback, teaching several new movements, or a first session back. (Formal re-tests & reprogramming are a reassessment — see Stage 03.)

NDIS follow-ups can also run 60 min where the client is receptive to a session of that length and it warrants the extra time.

Follow-up frequency
CadenceStreamsWhy
Weekly RSL · DVA · NDIS Keep an ongoing progression & monitoring rhythm.
Fortnightly / monthly Medicare · Private · Silverchain Judge by likely adherence to the prescription and their confidence executing it solo.
Stage 03

Reassessment

Formal re-testing to show progress and re-direct the program.

When — 8–12 weeks from the previous assessment. A formal re-test and re-direct of the program.

In the session
  1. Re-test with VALD to show improved measures.
  2. Check in on their goals and physical capacity.
  3. Realign the next steps — re-program from the new results (repeating process as done in Stage 01: program design).
  4. Book the follow-up for the following week to run through the new program (as per Stage 02).
After the session
  1. Create a GP letter — after every reassessment. DVA · Medicare · Private (if GP referred) · Silverchain
Stage 04

Between Reassessments

The maintenance loop — keeping the client progressing until their next reassessment falls due.

Ongoing
  1. Continue follow-up consults at the set frequency (see Stage 02) until the next reassessment.
  2. The next reassessment falls due 8–12 weeks on (Stage 03) — then the cycle repeats.
  3. At each reassessment, use your clinical reasoning to judge whether the client is ready to be discharged. If they are, that's their final reassessment — proceed to Stage 05.
Stage 05

Final Consult / Discharge

Ending a capped referral cycle with a final consult and discharge — or keeping ongoing-stream clients in care.

When — by stream
StreamDischarge approach
Medicare · Silverchain Run a final consult on the last session of the referral cycle — for Silverchain, the 3rd — then discharge (see the paths below).
Private No referral cycle — discharge when EP service is no longer clinically necessary. If not already done when they're cleared, direct them into the gym ecosystem.
DVA · RSL · NDIS Aim to keep them as ongoing clients — maintain regular reassessment and GP communication rather than discharging.
Discharge test — only discharge once you're certain the client would not keep benefiting from ongoing progression and monitoring.
On the final consult — direct their next step
  • Direct them into the gym ecosystem, following the marketing guidelines, or
  • Clear them to return to their pre-injury duties.
After the final consult
  1. Send the GP letter to the referring GP. DVA · Medicare · Private (if GP referred) · Silverchain
